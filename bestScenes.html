Here are few best stranger thing's scenes:

Running up that Hill - Max :

The iconic scene where Max Mayfield runs to escape Vecna occurs in Stranger Things Season 4, Episode 4, titled "Dear Billy". While often described as the Upside Down, this sequence actually takes place entirely within Vecna's Mind Lair, a fragmented, dark red psychological realm where he traps his victims' consciousness.


Never Ending Story-Dustin and Suzie :

The "Turn around" scene from Stranger Things refers to the iconic moment in the Season 3 finale ("Chapter Eight: The Battle of Starcourt") where Dustin Henderson and his girlfriend Suzie sing a duet of theme song from "The NeverEnding Story". I believe :

The "I Believe" scene refers to the highly emotional final scene of the Stranger Things Season 5 series finale. In this concluding sequence, the remaining characters repeatedly utter the phrase "I believe" as a powerful call to hope and resistance. They choose to hold onto the belief that Eleven is still alive and out there somewhere, even after she seemingly disappears into a portal to close the bridge between worlds.


El-Max Scenes :

The term "El-Max" (or Elmax) refers to the iconic friendship between Eleven (El) and Max Mayfield in the hit Netflix Official Site series, Stranger Things. While their bond gets off to a rocky start in Season 2, they evolve into one of the most empowering and beloved dynamics in the entire show.


Eleven Closing the gate and sacrifising herself :

In the Stranger Things Season 5 finale, Eleven (El) heroically sacrifices herself by choosing to stay inside the disintegrating Upside Down. Knowing that the military intends to capture and exploit her powers, she detaches herself from her friends, faces the collapsing dimension, and ultimately disappears in a whirlwind to end the dark cycle permanently.
